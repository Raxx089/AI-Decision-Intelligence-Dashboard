AI-Powered Enterprise Decision Intelligence Dashboard
B2B SaaS UX Case Study
1. Project Overview

An enterprise-grade AI-powered Decision Intelligence platform that helps executives, operations leaders, finance teams, and product stakeholders make faster, data-backed decisions using:

AI-generated insights
Predictive analytics
Real-time operational dashboards
Conversational analytics
Root-cause detection
Automated recommendations
Cross-functional collaboration

The platform combines traditional BI dashboards with AI copilots and decision workflows.

2. Problem Statement

Enterprise organizations face major challenges:

Too much fragmented data across systems
Slow executive decision-making
Teams relying on manual reporting
Lack of predictive insights
Dashboard overload
Non-technical users unable to analyze data
Poor collaboration between business and data teams

Traditional BI tools are visualization-focused, not decision-focused.

Modern organizations need:

AI-assisted decision-making
Real-time intelligence
Natural language analytics
Predictive recommendations
Cross-platform orchestration

Industry trends show a strong shift toward AI-native business intelligence platforms and conversational analytics.
